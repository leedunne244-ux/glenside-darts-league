OFFICIAL COMPETITION GUIDE

Rules & Guide

2026 SEASON
League dates

Thursday 19 November, 26 November, 3 December and 10 December 2026. Grand Finals Night: Thursday 17 December 2026.

League format

Round robin. Every player plays each other once. Two dartboards. All matches are 501, straight in, double out, first to 2 legs (best of 3).

League points

Win 2–0 or 2–1: 3 points. Lose 2–1: 1 point. Lose 2–0: 0 points. Walkover win: 2 points; walkover loss: 0.

League rankings

Points, matches won, leg difference, legs won, then head-to-head. If still tied, a first-to-3-legs rematch decides the ranking.

Match rules

Three darts per visit. Players must finish on a double; bullseye counts. A bust restores the score at the start of that visit. Throw for bull to determine who starts; alternate starters each leg.

Attendance & rearranged matches

Players should make every effort to attend all four nights. Matches may be played on alternative nights, with results sent to the organiser. All league matches must be completed before Finals Night. A 2-point walkover may be awarded where reasonable attempts to arrange a fixture have failed.

Grand Finals Night: Cup & Shield

Thursday 17 December 2026. Cup: league positions 1st–8th. Quarter-finals: 1st v 8th, 4th v 5th, 2nd v 7th, 3rd v 6th. Quarter-finals and semi-finals first to 3 legs (best of 5); final first to 4 legs (best of 7). Shield: positions 9th–16th. Quarter-finals: 9th v 16th, 12th v 13th, 10th v 15th, 11th v 14th. Quarter-finals and semi-finals first to 1 leg; final first to 2 legs (best of 3). Winners advance to the next round.
